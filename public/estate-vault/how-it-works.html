How Estate Vault works

Estate Vault does not interpret your will or give legal advice. You decide what must be done. Use it to record property, money, decisions, receipts, communications, and distributions as the estate progresses.

Eight steps, one record

You do not need to finish everything at once. Work through inventory → money → sales (if any) → distributions → exports as the estate moves forward.

  1. 1
    Set up the estate

    Create the case, admin access, and basic probate details.

  2. 2
    Record will instructions yourself

    Enter gifts, people, and notes. The app does not decide them for you.

  3. 3
    Inventory property

    Rooms, photos, status, and reference codes in one place.

  4. 4
    Track money in and out

    Funds, expenses, debts, and PR advances with Cash available totals.

  5. 5
    Document decisions

    Keep notes and history so you can explain what happened later.

  6. 6
    Communicate with beneficiaries

    Invite heirs, send Family Updates, and keep messaging in the record.

  7. 7
    Record distributions & receipts

    Cash and property distributions with acknowledgements.

  8. 8
    Export the final record

    Court-supporting packs and formal accounting for counsel to review.

Start documenting

Open the app to create your first estate. Review exports with your attorney before filing anything with the court.
