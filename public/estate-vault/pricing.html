Pricing for Estate Vault

Billed per estate (per case), not per heir or helper. Pay only while an estate is open.

$29.99 / month per estate

What you get

  • 14-day free trial on your first estate as Personal Representative
  • $29.99/month after the trial to keep that estate open
  • Additional estates: $29.99/month each, with a 7-day grace period to subscribe (no second free trial)
  • Family, helpers, and public sales are always free with paid PR access
  • Cancel in the Stripe customer portal when an estate is finished

If billing lapses

There is a 7-day grace period with reminders. Then Personal Representative, family, helper, and auction access for that estate pause until renewed. Settings → Billing shows status and Subscribe / Manage billing.

Checkout may show the Stripe business name (for example FRPL) while the product line says Estate Vault Standard.
