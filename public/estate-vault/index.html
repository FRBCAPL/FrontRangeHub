Who it is for

Family Personal Representatives and executors who need one place to inventory estate property, track funds and expenses, communicate with heirs, and export court-supporting reports — without turning notes into a maze of spreadsheets.

  • Inventory property with photos and room organization
  • Track money in and out with a clear Cash available picture
  • Invite family and helpers with role-based access
  • Record distributions with receipts and export reports for counsel

What it is not

Estate Vault is an organization and documentation tool. It is not a law firm, tax service, court e-filing system, CPA, or any licensed professional replacement. You do not need to upload the will, death certificate, or Letters. We do not ask for bank account numbers, social security numbers, or other sensitive information.

Ready when you are

Your first estate includes a 14-day free trial, then $29.99/month per estate. Family, helpers, and public sales stay free with paid PR access.
